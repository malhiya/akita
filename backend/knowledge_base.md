[medication] Medication and supplements are non-negotiable priority. Default duration 5 minutes. Give at the same time every day.
[vet] Vet visits and checkups are non-negotiable priority. Default duration 60 minutes. One-time unless stated.
[feeding] Feeding is high priority. Default duration 10 minutes. Adult dogs and cats are usually fed twice daily.
[feeding] Puppies and kittens under 6 months usually need 3 to 4 meals a day.
[walk] Walks are high priority. Default duration 30 minutes. Most dogs need at least one walk daily.
[grooming] Grooming (brushing, baths, nails) is medium priority. Default duration 20 minutes. Usually weekly.
[play] Play and enrichment are low priority. Default duration 20 minutes.
[training] Training is low priority. Default duration 15 minutes. Short daily sessions work best.
[time] "Morning" means 08:00, "afternoon" 14:00, "evening" 18:00, "night" 21:00.
[health] If health notes mention diabetes, kidney disease, or similar, treat feeding and medication as non-negotiable.
